## Digest

**Recurring ideas.** (1) Tokyo 9:55 fix and Gotobi dominate JP work. Effects are significant only on Gotobi days and are weaker outside USDJPY (JM05, JM06, JM08). (2) Session-dependent behaviour: fade in Asia, follow in London/NY. (3) Prior-day high/low breakouts. (4) Carry and swap holding. (5) Most EA writing is about validation: forward/OOS, walk-forward, parameter plateaus, Monte Carlo.

**Promising new testable rules:**
- **Weekend-gap fade (JM11).** Fade the Monday-open gap toward Friday's close with a stop beyond 1× gap. Claimed reason: thin Sunday liquidity, and roughly 80% of gaps fill.
- **Month-end 10:00–15:00 JST window for USDJPY on days 29–31 (JM03).** Claimed reason: importer settlement flow.
- **13:45–14:45 JST JPY-weak drift (JM01).**
- **Round-number logic (JM02).** Use breakout through 00/50 rather than fading it.
- **Gotobi effect by pair (JM06).** Check that the effect scales USDJPY > EURJPY > EURUSD. This tests the flow explanation.
- **Overnight vs intraday split on indices (JM21).**
- **Vol-regime switch on XAUUSD (JE04).** For example, an ATR percentile selects breakout vs mean-reversion.
- **Pullback scalper plus "trend-end" fade companion (JE18).** Hedges the many-small-wins-then-one-big-loss tail.
- **Range-based vol estimators (JM24)** for sizing and regime filters.

**Negative results worth avoiding:** no post-FOMC next-morning drift (JM15); plain non-Gotobi fix trades have no edge; October yen-weak and August yen-strong seasonality are not significant (JM09, JM18); Tokyo RSI/BB fades lost in 2024–25 (JM17).

**Failure modes practitioners report:**
- Edges decay after about 2022–23: anomaly EA recent PF ≈1.01; ChatGPT EA PF fell from 1.31 to ≈1.05 (JE07, JE21).
- Early-morning spread widening kills morning scalpers (JE15, JE16).
- DST misalignment breaks session rules (JE13).
- Nanpin/martingale and pullback-scalp tail blow-ups.
- Mass-generated EAs: about 30% survive forward testing (JE12).
- ML leakage and non-stationary features (JE02, JE03).
- Too-low spread assumptions (JM14).
- Lot escalation after good runs (JE24).
