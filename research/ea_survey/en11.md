# EA survey, slice en11: long-running MQL5 signals, open-source EAs with forward results, and individual algo-FX journals (ENGLISH)

Status: 50 rows, read on 2026-11-04. Method: WebFetch of every page (50 of 50 bodies read; no row is excerpt only). MQL5 signals were picked from the MT4 and MT5 signal lists sorted by weeks (page 1 and 2), then each signal page was read. GitHub repos were found with the GitHub REST search and READMEs were read. Blogs and forums were found with 11 WebSearch calls. Author types: 46 rows are individuals or forums (92%); 4 rows are vendor-individual or other (8%: EN11-30, 32, 38, 45). Caps: mql5.com 25 rows, github.com 7 rows. No URL repeats seen_urls.txt or deep1, jp4, jp5, en8, en9, en10.

Notes on MQL5 signal rows: "live duration" uses the "Weeks" field (weeks / 4.35). On many pages the "Started" date is much later than the weeks imply (for example 2026 with 300+ weeks); we use weeks and say so. Signal pages do not show a Real/Demo label in the fetched text; the server name is given instead. A GitHub issue (EN11-30) says paid MQL5 signals must be real broker accounts. Balance DD and equity DD often differ a lot; we give both. Large deposits and withdrawals make growth % and balance DD unreliable (EN11-16, EN11-19, EN11-25).

## Digest

- Long life on the MQL5 list does not mean edge. Many 5-11 year signals end near PF 1.0: EN11-01 (night scalper, PF 1.00), EN11-14 (10 years, PF 0.98), EN11-20 (8 years, PF 1.01), EN11-05 (breakout, PF 1.04), EN11-17 (no-grid SR system, PF 1.05).
- Verified long failures are visible if you look past the top of the list: EN11-06 (-66.81%, 97% DD, same EA family as winning accounts), EN11-09 (-64.61%), EN11-10 (-66.17%), EN11-11 (-55.16%), EN11-12 (-61.90%).
- Night scalpers: still alive but thin. EN11-02 PF 1.06 with 48% balance DD; EN11-03 states a rollover "virtual stop loss" and copier slippage of about 1.4 pips; EN11-33 shows a year of Megadroid gains lost in one evening.
- The cleanest long records are low-frequency crosses with SL and few deposits: EN11-18 (EURAUD/AUDCAD, +351% in about 94 months, 12% balance DD, no top-ups) and EN11-22 (many pairs, PF 1.49).
- Quiet-cross mean reversion (NZDCAD, AUDCAD) shows again, as in Waka Waka: EN11-16 (PF 3.84), EN11-24 (AUDCAD hedging, but 85% equity DD). EN11-16 also has huge deposit and withdrawal flows, so its low DD cannot be trusted.
- Gold: the multi-year winners are mostly long-biased in a gold bull market (EN11-07, EN11-08 99.9% long, EN11-25 manual). EN11-39 shows a gold breakout backtest (+99%) that lost to buy-and-hold (+142%). Our gold tests should use buy-and-hold as the baseline.
- Costs decide results. EN11-26 (live OANDA): win rate 78% but net loss; gross Sharpe 1.26 becomes 0.03 net. EN11-29: a fee filter turns London ORB from -35% to +3%. EN11-27: gold M1 mean reversion lost under real ticks. EN11-34: a 75% win-rate EA made money only through rebates.
- Breakout systems on majors give back gains in range months and break on regime change: EN11-36 (Aug 2011, false breakouts), EN11-37 (Atipaq +100% in Mar 2012 to zero by Sep, blamed on the SNB EURCHF floor), EN11-05 (PF 1.04 over 5 years).
- Grid and recovery: EN11-04 (grid, 5 years, equity DD 42% vs balance DD 19%), EN11-38 (EURCHF grid survived the SNB gap only through low leverage), EN11-47/48/49 (forum recovery and basket grids: no verified edge, pips without DD).
- Individual life cycle: EN11-33 to EN11-37 show one trader's EA portfolio: +16.94% (2011, partly rebates and bonuses), then -26% (2012, "every Forex account lost money"), then exit from FX.
- Screening rules that work: ignore growth when deposits are added, require age, and judge growth against DD (EN11-28); MQL5 Signals are broker-fed, unlike Collective2 paper records (EN11-30).
