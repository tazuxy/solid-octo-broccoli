## Digest

- **Gotobi live result is a large loss (JP4-01, JP4-02, JP4-03, JP4-55, JP4-66).** One developer's free Gotobi EA (buy 00:30, exit 9:55, reversal short to 14:55, 50-pip stops) had a 10-year backtest PF of 1.34, but the live account went from 1,000,000 yen (2026-01-08) to 525,400 (May), 594,600 (July), 394,700 (Sep) and 154,500 yen (Oct). Losses came in the 2026 intervention months and in one-way yen moves; single news days hit the 50-pip stop.
- **Fix-minute costs, measured (JP4-58, JP4-61).** On real OANDA accounts about 80% of backtest profit was kept. Slippage at 9:55 was 0.5-2.1 pips per leg, against 0.1-0.2 pips for the overnight entry. The spread spike is at 9:53-9:54 (0.93 pips in 2024, about 3x normal). Our Gotobi test should add about 0.5-2 pips per fix leg.
- **Post-fix sell beats pre-fix buy (JP4-47, JP4-48, JP4-15).** In a 2010-2025 M1 study, buy 9:00 to 9:55 had PF 1.34 (0.98 at 1.75-pip cost); sell 9:55 to 15:00 had PF 1.68 (1.40 at 1.75 pips). The 5th was the only losing Gotobi day. Another test says the fix effect broke down after June 2022. An earlier (0:00) entry beat later entries.
- **Fix/anomaly EAs in real portfolios do poorly (JP4-11, JP4-42, JP4-63, JP4-22).** Two anomaly EAs had live PF 0.91 and 0.73 on one user's account; a EURJPY Gotobi/Friday EA was negative in 2025 and 2026; a Nakane EA added in spring 2025 was gone by year-end. The best case: a 5-year GogoJungle demo forward kept about 60% of backtest profit, and 2 extra pips of spread would remove it.
- **Rollover and morning scalping depend on cost (JP4-51, JP4-32, JP4-23, JP4-54).** GBPAUD spread averaged 18.2 pips in the 0:00 hour versus 3.7-4.1 pips in the day; the same EA earned +1.3 pips/trade on a low-spread account and -0.5 on a high-spread one. XM limited IB terms for a 0:00 scalper. A gold morning scalp failed on a real account within 6 months.
- **Calendar flows fade (JP4-34, JP4-44, JP4-45).** Month-end London-fix GBP buy: PF 1.36 (2015-2024) but 1.04 (2021-2024), before costs. Monday gap fill: 76% wins in 38 real trades, but average loss about 2x average win, and one month took half the year's profit. Tokyo-range London breakout: PF 1.14 and negative in 2024.
- **Portfolio survivorship (JP4-09, JP4-57, JP4-10, JP4-52).** One blogger's 2025 portfolio made -20.3 pips, but the table of EAs still running showed +3,173 pips. Almost none of his 18 EAs from Jan 2021 survived to 2026. EAs chosen for strong recent forwards failed within weeks; removing the bottom 5 of 35 EAs after the fact doubled a month's return.
- **Grid/martingale end states (JP4-06, JP4-16, JP4-17, JP4-59, JP4-60, JP4-70).** Repeated -99.9% stop-outs on FOMC/NFP days; a 4.5-year individual record with one -4.6M-yen year and two wipe-outs; gold nanpin wiped on an event day; an AUDCAD nanpin EA tripled in 22 months, then its DD passed the margin limit. Profit withdrawal is the only survival tool that the authors name.
- **Live DD beats backtest DD (JP4-13, JP4-14, JP4-18).** A nanpin EA passed its 16-year backtest max DD within months; a 96.88%-win EA lost PF from 3.19 to 1.86 with about 1 pip more spread; a popular GBPUSD time-window martingale hit about 30% DD in 8 months for +17%.
- **Broker and infrastructure risk (JP4-43, JP4-67, JP4-53, JP4-07).** The お名前.com VPS hack (2026-10-21) stopped EAs for two bloggers in this slice and caused unauthorized-trade losses of up to about 6M yen. The same EA in the same month made +29,050 yen at one broker and -127,811 at another.
- **Long personal series show yearly givebacks (JP4-25, JP4-26, JP4-56, JP4-73, JP4-24, JP4-37).** A developer with 9 profitable years still gave back most of each year's gains at some point. Other individuals report two losing years in a row, or EAs that weakened right after the lot size was raised.
- **Weak or promotional claims to discount (JP4-12, JP4-38, JP4-46, JP4-68).** Claims of +122%/year or +70% in 2 months come with no account link; treat them as marketing.
