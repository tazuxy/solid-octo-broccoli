Status: 80 rows (EN10-01 to EN10-80). Pages read on 2026-11-04. Method: 19 WebSearch calls to find sources, then WebFetch on every row, so all 80 rows are body-read (100%). Many pages were read on more than one page (first page and last page of long threads) to find the later fate. WebFetch was blocked (HTTP 403 or tool refusal) on forexfactory, reddit, babypips, myfxbook, forexpeacearmy, trade2win, forex-station and medium, so those sites are not used. Sources used instead: stevehopwoodforex.com forum (11), mql5.com forum and blogs (11), elitetrader.com (11), Trustpilot buyer reviews (12, counted as review site), forexsb.com forum (10), strategyquant.com forum (7), earnforex.com forum (7), Substack (6), mechanicalforex.com (2), GitHub (2), Zorro forum (1). No domain has more than 12 rows. Author types: forum 32, individual 30, review site 12, vendor-individual 6. Official or vendor rows: 6 of 80 (7.5%: EN10-16, 17, 32, 33, 53, 64). Individuals, forums and independent reviewers: 74 of 80 (92.5%). No URL repeats seen_urls.txt or the other slices checked (jp4, jp5, en8, en9, deep1). Numbers are copied from the pages; "not stated" means the page had no figure. Most results are self-reported; "live verified" means the page links a myfxbook, MQL5 signal or broker statement, not that we checked it.

## Digest

- **Independent testers drop most EAs fast.** Forum testers who ran dozens of free EAs side by side removed most within weeks or months; numbers are thin, and few tests end with a final result (EN10-01, EN10-02, EN10-04, EN10-42).
- **Generated (data-mined) EAs: about half or more fail forward.** In a counted demo incubator, 31 of 59 backtest-positive EAs lost money (EN10-48). Without a strict filter only 33% were profitable; with an SQN filter 55% (EN10-59). Live, 13 of 24 EAs (54%) were in profit, yet the portfolio made +19.9% with PF 1.35 in 6 months (EN10-58).
- **Top performers do not stay on top.** Only about 21% of new "top band" EAs were still top after 6 months (EN10-60). Only 2 of 700+ EAs stayed "elite" over six monthly checks (EN10-61). Picking last week's best EAs failed (EN10-47).
- **Backtest to live haircut.** One private EA made 18% live versus 28% in the backtest on the same 282 trades (EN10-12). A "100% accurate" workflow was "destroyed by SLIPPAGES" live (EN10-56). In a scalper dispute, 24 of 55 trades differed from the tester (EN10-09). Under 10% of mined strategies keep their backtest behaviour live (EN10-52).
- **Indicator EAs (MA cross, MACD, HGI, Super Slope, Awesome) show no lasting edge.** Coders said "defeated, unable to produce a profitable bot" (EN10-41) and "not a winner for sure yet" (EN10-39). A gold MA-cross EA with filters gave back its gains and then declined (EN10-38). A 9/21 EMA cross went from +15% to -51% after costs (EN10-76). Win rates above 50% still lost money (EN10-04).
- **SMC and supply-demand EAs have no forward records.** The only cases are a vendor's single 5-day FTMO pass (EN10-32) and a live zone EA that never posted results (EN10-40).
- **ML and AI EAs: coin-flip level out of sample.** Gradient boosting reached AUC 0.51 on gold, and a coin-flip entry control did about as well (EN10-29). Reinforcement learning found more "systems" on random data than on real data (EN10-36). LLM backtests can be memory, not skill (EN10-34). Buyers say Market ML EAs are "history readers" (EN10-74). Only 2 of 5 AI-built strategies matched their backtests, over a few weeks (EN10-72).
- **Prop firms: rules, not edge, decide many outcomes.** Problems include trailing drawdown (EN10-13), the consistency rule (EN10-70), order-modify warnings and a firm shutdown (MFF) (EN10-63), and EA detection bans (EN10-64). A long-tested robust portfolio earned only about 0.3% a month, against targets of 8-10% (EN10-51). Buyer reviews of a prop-firm EA show blown accounts and no clear pass (EN10-62).
- **Commercial EA fates from buyer reviews.** The usual pattern is early praise, then blow-ups, then silence or a closed website: ForexRobotTrader (EN10-22), WallStreet and Forex Gold Investor (EN10-23), GPS Forex Robot (EN10-24), 1000pip Climber (site closed, EN10-26), iQ EA (two $170k accounts wiped out in one day, EN10-67), Signal2Forex (EN10-80).
- **"EA review" sites are not independent.** One review site sells its own robot (EN10-27). Several products show only 5-star reviews without numbers (EN10-26, EN10-28).
- **Execution and broker setup can turn a tested system into a loss.** Examples: a wrong asset file (Zorro Z12, PF 0.287 live, EN10-50), order-rate limits (EN10-79), broker spread changes that ended scalpers (EN10-06), and no trades at all (EN10-49).
- **Fit for our tests.** All of this supports our findings: simple indicator and breakout rules fail after costs and out of sample (EN10-35, EN10-77). The only positive live results are thin (PF about 1.35) and come from diversified portfolios with strict filters (EN10-58, EN10-33).
