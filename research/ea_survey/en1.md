## Digest

Coverage: 60 excerpt-based entries (target 100; search budget exhausted, Reddit unreachable).

1. **Long verified live records (≥12 months).** Four clear cases: Waka Waka (#18, grid, MQL5 since 2018); Night Hunter Pro (#6/#7, night scalping on CHF/CAD crosses, from ~2020); the Asia mean-reversion signal #13 (since 2020); Evening Scalper (#43, from ~2022, and #42 cites ~49 months on Myfxbook). NightWalker (#11) is possible, but its duration is unclear. The dominant logic is **Asian-session / rollover mean reversion**. The single longest record is a **grid**.
2. **Common settings.** Pairs: EURCHF, GBPCAD, EURCAD, EURAUD, AUDCAD, USDCHF, GBPCHF, EURGBP, AUDNZD/NZDCAD. Timeframe M15. Entries from late NY to early Tokyo, with exit before London. Small TP and wider SL (one record has avg win 5.5 pips vs avg loss 6.4). ECN brokers (IC Markets, Pepperstone, Fusion). Several EAs ship broker-specific set files. One survivor explicitly **skips 00:00-01:00 GMT rollover**. Vendors say "no grid", except Waka Waka and Asian Scalper.
3. **Fates.** Decay is the norm. The #13 author admits the edge may be lost; Night Hunter Pro "took a hit" after ~2022 and has blow-up complaints; Megadroid-era Asian-range EAs faded; Myfxbook PFs sit at 1.02-1.10 after commissions. The extreme case is blow-up on EURCHF tail events (SNB 2015: stops filled ~1,950 pips away). Some vendors delete accounts (survivorship bias).
4. **What our tests may have missed.** Modelling rollover spread spikes (stops hit by a widened ask, not by price). A rollover-exclusion window. Pair-level costs in Asia (EURCHF/EURGBP widen most; a live EURCHF loss in #12). Copy-trade latency vs local execution (#7). Larger-TP variants (>20 pips) being less broker-sensitive. Demo PF overstates live PF.
5. **Counts.**

   - By author type: vendor-individual 24; individual 13; review site 11; forum 9; company 3 (official/marketing ≈5%). Total 60.
