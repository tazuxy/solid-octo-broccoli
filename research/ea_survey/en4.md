## Digest

(1) **Long verified live records (≥12 months):** only one in this slice. Waka Waka (#2, MQL5 signal since 2018, ~90 months, +329%) is **grid-averaged BB/RSI mean reversion**. No pure mean-reversion EA (RSI(2), BB, divergence, CCI, stochastic) and no price-action EA (pin bar, engulfing, S/R, S/D, SMC/ICT) showed a ≥12-month verified record.

(2) **Settings of the long-lived one:** AUDCAD/NZDCAD/AUDNZD (range-bound commodity crosses), M15, RSI + BB reversal entries, grid adds, no fixed tight SL. Its stated risks are grid DD above prop-firm limits and VPS/connection failure.

(3) **Fates:** decay (Night Hunter Pro #38), vendor out of business (OnControl Night Scalper #36, PF 1.25), blow-up (Scalping-ProSystem #37), still negative (Bollinger Reversion #1, -7%). Most others have no record at all. An 800-EA real-tick audit (#9) found none with a real live track record.

(4) **What our tests may have missed:**
- MR seems to survive live only on low-volatility crosses, and only with grid averaging.
- BB applied to RSI values, with bandwidth/ATR filters (#11).
- Broker-feed dependence of oscillator thresholds (#12).
- A weekly-S&R gate for pin bars, plus closing before the weekend (#29).
- Exit logic dominates CCI/MR results (#10).
- Execution costs kill sub-minute MR (#35).

The RSI(2)-on-FX failure is **confirmed** by an independent 10-year EURUSD D1 backtest (-4.5%, #14) and by vendor admissions (#13, #15). Mechanized order blocks broke even at best in forex (#25).
