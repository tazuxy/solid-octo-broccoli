## Digest

(1) **Long verified live records (12 months or more): none found.** The only verified live record with a visible result is the FXGOLD currency basket (MQL5 signal): −57% in under 12 months. Three other MQL5 signals (news grid, SwapSlap carry, Impossible Gold) showed no figures in the excerpts. English sources for these topics are mostly vendor backtests.

(2) **Common settings.** News straddles place stop pairs about 100 points out, 2–5 minutes before the release, cancel after about 10 minutes and use a max-spread filter. Gap fades trigger at 10 pips or more and trade toward the fill. Gold NY-open breakouts use a 09:30–09:45 or 09:30–10:00 range on M5/M30 with 0.75R–2.5R targets and fixed SL. Carry EAs take the swap-positive side, often with a grid.

(3) **Fates.** Developers call news straddles "dead" because spreads widen to 100+ pips at release. A news robot went out of business, and a failed Myfxbook account was removed. HSBC says month-end "stopped working", and the Barclays last-day window is decaying. The strength basket fell 57%.

(4) **What our tests may have missed:**
- Month-end entries a few days early, not only the last-day fix (Barclays).
- Pre-fix drift followed by post-fix reversal (academic WMR study).
- Feeds handle weekend gaps differently. A 78–81% fill rate is not profit.
- Round numbers combined with prior S/R.
- Spread filters and DST-correct session clocks.
- News-EA bans at prop firms.

(5) **Counts (69 of 100; the search budget ran out).**
- By evidence: none 45; backtest only 13; demo forward 1; live unverified 5; live verified 5 (MQL5 signal 4, FXBlue 1).
- By author type: vendor-individual 18; forum 17; company 17; individual 12 (2 academic); review site 3; broker 2. Company plus broker is 28%, above the 15% target.
