No row is excerpt-only. WebSearch calls used: 27 of 30. Method: a few searches to find sources, then reading thread lists, blog archives (WordPress API, Blogger search) and review indexes, and following links. No URL from seen_urls.txt, en1-en8, en10, en11, jp1-jp5 or deep1 is repeated (3 rows that overlapped with en10, written at the same time, were replaced).

So the reddit/FF/babypips journals asked for are missing. I did not use mirrors. The main sources instead are individuals' blogs (Rob Carver, Mechanical Forex/Asirikuy), Elite Trader and MQL5 threads, MQL5 signal pages, and independent reviewers that check vendors' public live records (The Robotic Trader, FxToplist demo tests).

## Digest

- **The "longest grid survivor" needs a correction.** Waka Waka's main account has a balance DD of 13.88% but an **equity DD of 72.42%** (Apr-May 2024). $3,000 was added during that DD. MQL5 stopped new subscriptions in Sep 2026, and one user account went to zero in Feb 2022 (EN9-60, EN9-70). In SUMMARY.md, Waka Waka should be described as "survived with a near-wipeout", not as a safe survivor.
- **One grid EA, very different results.** Perceptrader AI: four real accounts with max DD of 22%, 23%, 65% and 69%, depending on settings and start date (EN9-61). Quantum Queen: one user lost $10,000 in 12 days, while other users made +244% to +2,093% (EN9-71). FXQuake: vendor real account +800%, independent demo -52% (EN9-41). For grids, start date and lot size decide the result more than the logic does.
- **Independent forward tests of sold EAs are mostly negative.** FxToplist: of 109 demo tests run for 365 days or more, 71 ended at 0% or below, 16 lost 80% or more, and the median max DD was 48.5% (EN9-40). The Robotic Trader: 19 of 25 EAs were Not Recommended after their own live records were checked (EN9-57). AlgoVerdict: 73 of 131 EAs have no live record at all (EN9-68).
- **High win rate does not protect the account.** Forex Fury's gold account won 57 of 58 trades and lost 96.10% (EN9-67). GPS Forex Robot: 91% winners and a max DD of 89.97% after a 7x "recovery" trade (EN9-65). "No stop-loss" averaging account: 71% winners, PF 0.87 (EN9-31). This matches our finding that win rate is misleading.
- **The only non-grid records that held up 2-3 years are gold breakout or short-term gold trend EAs:** Gold Trade Pro (PF 1.59, 3 years; EN9-59), Gold Reaper (PF 2.09, 2 years; EN9-63), Happy Gold at 2% risk (PF 4.71, 31 months; EN9-64). But profit sits in a few days (84% of profit from 7 of 225 days, EN9-59), and much of the edge may be gold's uptrend: Mad Turtle's own backtest is flat from 2005 to 2014 (EN9-77). Our gold tests should check for this long bias.
- **Range breakout on FX is thin live, as in our tests.** Range Breakout EA: PF 1.16, about $1.35 per trade, and 16 of 1,849 entries made more than all of the profit (EN9-58). Apex Drawdown Zero: gold PF 3.40, but EURUSD PF 0.90 and EURJPY PF 0.46 (EN9-09). USDJPY M5 breakout: +4,000% in backtest, then not tradable after a spread change and a code bug (EN9-82).
- **Trend following live is lumpy, with a Sharpe of about 0.6-0.8.** Rob Carver's 11-year live futures record: yearly +58%, +23%, -14%, -4%, +5% ... -16%; 9-year Sharpe 0.58, 11-year CAGR 12.0% (EN9-06/07/17/34/35/47/48). His in-sample backtest Sharpe was 1.31 (EN9-83). FX Donchian on majors (Quimichi) had a 2,127-day DD, then +60% in 2014 (EN9-21). Four FX systems that worked for 3-5 years all failed at about the same time (EN9-03, EN9-24).
- **How backtest and live differ, with numbers.** Live costs were close to the simulation in Carver's year 1 (slippage 17bp vs 120bp simulated), but about 3x the backtest after a model change in year 8 (EN9-06, EN9-48). Asirikuy, over 1M live trades: -1.37 pips per trade on average, but GBPJPY was 8.78 pips worse; 400 systems were dropped when the simulated spread was raised (EN9-19, EN9-20). Live volatility was about double the backtest (EN9-36). An EOD mean-reversion system kept only a quarter of its per-trade edge live (EN9-37). Bar data and tick data can give opposite results for the same mined strategy (EN9-51).
- **Live Sharpe haircut and decay base rates.** Live Sharpe was 15-25% below backtest (EN9-56). Under 40% of contest algos matched their backtest over 4 live months (EN9-55). Most mined-system failures came in the first 200 live days (EN9-49). In-sample profit statistics did not predict live profit, but risk statistics carried over (correlation above 0.78; EN9-25, EN9-50).
- **Carry is very weak after 2016.** A monthly top-3 swap basket made 1.7%/yr unlevered, lost money in 6 of 11 years, and at 10:1 leverage lost 43% after being down about 95% (EN9-72).
- **Survival tools named by practitioners:** hard equity/basket stop, fixed lots instead of multipliers, one live account per EA version, retiring a system when live results leave the Monte Carlo band or reach 2-2.5x backtest DD, and withdrawing profit (EN9-05, EN9-09, EN9-25, EN9-53, EN9-80). The failure pattern for no-SL grids is "months or years of small wins, then one trend" (EN9-22: none of the top long-term myfxbook accounts from 2014 was still alive in 2016).
- **Public journals usually end without a final report** (EN9-16, EN9-26, EN9-38), and sellers drop products instead of posting a post-mortem (EN9-68, EN9-69). Public records are biased toward survivors.
