## Digest

(1) **Long verified live records (12 months or more).** None surfaced in this slice. No entry had a Myfxbook, FXBlue, MQL5-signal or GogoJungle forward page with 12 months or more shown in the excerpt. The only multi-year live records are unverified individual reports of repeat or grid trading: the Toraripi "1億" blogger (#30), and the Flex EA real account, which ran about 12-15 months to end-2022 (#5).

(2) **Common settings.** Gold dominates the newer EAs (XAUUSD on M15/H1). Breakouts use the Asian range broken at the London open (#16, #17). Grid or DCA is the default for gold, often long-only (#11, #13, #18, #24). Repeat grids (Toraripi, Triauto, Loop) use wide ranges with small fixed take-profits. Explicit "no martingale" claims appear on breakout and trend EAs (#14, #22). Typical filters are RSI, standard deviation and news (FOMC).

(3) **Typical fates.** Blow-up or near blow-up for grid and nanpin: the Flex EA real account bust and -98.7% week (#5, #6), the Toraripi -94% in 2007 (#30), and a vendor that openly says its EA will go bust (#24). Short-lived gold signals carry high-growth warnings (#21). One London-breakout listing shows max DD 71% against ROI 20% (#17).

(4) **What our tests may have missed.**
- Event filters: an FOMC spike took a gold breakout-grid to a 25% drawdown (#10).
- Symbol and spread variants of gold (#27).
- Record keeping: verification tools that record only at position close hide floating drawdown (#28, #39).
- Execution: EAs halting or failing to close positions (#26), and loss-cut slippage (#49).
- The "withdraw profits, accept ruin" operating model (#24).
