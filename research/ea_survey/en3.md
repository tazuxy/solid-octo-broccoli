## Digest

These counts cover only the 40 entries collected before the shared search budget ran out. All are excerpt-based.

1. **Long verified records (≥12 months):** none is confirmed. Four MQL5 signals are live-verified (EN3-008 MA trend, since 2025; EN3-009 multi-pair trend; EN3-010 H4/D1 multi-pair, since 2024; EN3-005 turtle). Only EN3-010, and possibly EN3-008, could exceed 12 months, and the excerpts give neither duration nor DD. EN3-007 is also verified but its logic is unknown and its returns look grid-like.
2. **Settings:** trend and Donchian EAs use H4/D1 with long channels (140/70 with an EMA200 filter, EN3-014). Range-breakout EAs use stop orders at the Asian or London high/low and cancel at session end (EN3-016, EN3-018). Gold appears again and again (EN3-003, EN3-013, EN3-039). Grid or martingale "recovery" add-ons are common (EN3-015, EN3-028).
3. **Fates:** London breakout on EURUSD loses about 28% over 2016–2026 (EN3-021). Breakout systems turn breakeven or fail once live (EN3-019, EN3-030). Trend EAs spend long stretches losing (7 of 12 months in EN3-012).
4. **What our tests may have missed:** XAUUSD rather than FX majors; much longer Donchian channels with an EMA200 filter; multi-market diversification; evaluation windows long enough to cover a trend EA's flat years.

   - **By evidence:** live verified 5, live unverified 12, demo forward 1, backtest only 8, none 14.
   - **By author:** individual 10, vendor-individual 13, forum 8, review site 3, company 6 (15%), broker 0.
