## Digest

**(1) Long verified live records (12 months or more):** only one grid EA clearly qualifies: Waka Waka, with an MQL5 signal since 2018 (about 90 months). Perceptrader AI, from the same vendor, has about 2 years on Myfxbook. The other verified records all ended at 95–99.97% drawdown. The survivors are filtered, slow grids. Unfiltered gold martingales died.

**(2) Common settings:**
