## Digest

(1) **Long verified records (≥12 months)** are rare in this slice. The only ones are GogoJungle forward records, which run on demo accounts. They cover the Tokyo fix/Gotobi (Tokyo Chuo, 2,677 trades, PF 1.10; ゴトー日仲値ドル円EA, PF 1.04; a fix EA with ≥1 year) and early-morning JST multi-position scalping (Premia, 18 months, +59%). No ≥12-month Myfxbook record was found.

(2) **Common settings.** Gotobi/fix EAs trade USDJPY, buying from the night or 3:30 JST into 9:55, with an optional post-fix sell, and sometimes larger lots on Gotobi days and Fridays. Morning scalpers use USDJPY, EURAUD or gold crosses on M1 to M30, enter in the 0:00 to 7:00 JST hours with RSI/BB mean reversion, take 1 to 20 positions (stacking is common), use shallow SL, and target high win rates.
